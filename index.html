➤ NearShare LOCAL SHARING

Send files on your Wi‑Fi. No account needed.

Download NearShare to one Windows PC. It starts a local web page, shows a QR code, and lets phones and computers on the same network join and receive files.

Get NearShare for Windows

Download NearShare Portable

No installation is required. Extract the ZIP file and double-click Start NearShare.cmd.

How sharing works

  1. Download and extract NearShare Portable on one computer.
  2. Double-click Start NearShare.cmd. Your browser opens automatically.
  3. On the phone or other computer, join the same Wi‑Fi or hotspot and scan the QR code.
  4. Select a device, send files, and approve the download on the receiving device.

Files stay on your local network. Keep the NearShare window open while sharing.
